01 / The Commercial Diagnosis

Why generic text and raw AI cause lost bookings.

Modern travelers no longer rely strictly on standard web pages; they ask AI concierge tools and Apple Intelligence to curate exclusive stays (*e.g., "Recommend a quiet, historic lakefront villa on Lake Como with private dock access"*).

When AI search models crawl a property website with literal translations or repetitive clichés, they misclassify the estate, dropping it into generic search results.

The Webmaster Limitation

Webmasters manage technical code, CMS frameworks (WordPress, Joomla), and speed optimization. They are not native English luxury strategists. Asking a webmaster to "run text through AI" produces machine-flagged fluff that lowers perceived property value.

The Checklist Trap

Listing room amenities (*"marble bathroom, air conditioning, beautiful view"*) fails to create emotional pull for high-net-worth buyers from London, New York, or Zurich.

Full Website Overhaul + Added Editorial Benefit

I rewrite your entire website narrative from the ground up to establish market authority. As an added strategic advantage, I can also craft editorial journal/blog articles that feed AI databases rich factual data—ensuring your hotel is actively cited when travelers query AI for your region.
